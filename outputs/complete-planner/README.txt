FINPLANNER COMPLETE FINANCIAL PLANNER - PHASE 1

CONTENTS
1. FinPlanner-Complete-Financial-Planner.xlsx
   A connected 16-sheet planning workbook for cash flow, net worth, emergency
   reserves, goals, debt, insurance, retirement, investment allocation, tax
   readiness, annual review, actions and model checks.

2. FinPlanner-Complete-Planning-Guide.pdf
   A 12-page printable companion that explains the planning order and includes
   worksheets for the main financial decisions.

HOW TO START
- Keep a clean backup of the files.
- Open the workbook and begin with Start Here, Profile and Monthly Budget.
- Edit yellow cells with blue text. Green cells calculate automatically.
- Use the Checks sheet before relying on the Dashboard.
- Finish by selecting no more than three actions in the 90 Day Plan.

COMPATIBILITY
The XLSX file is designed for Microsoft Excel and can be imported into Google
Sheets. Some chart, font, validation or conditional-format details may vary.

PRIVACY
The files work on your device. Do not enter passwords, PINs, Aadhaar numbers,
full account numbers or sensitive identity documents.

IMPORTANT
These files are educational planning tools. They do not provide personalised
investment, tax, legal, insurance or accounting advice. Expected returns and
inflation are assumptions, not guarantees. Verify current laws, limits, product
terms and suitability independently.
